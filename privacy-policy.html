Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information CodeBridge Academy collects through this website, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

CodeBridge Academy is a software development company and practical technology training academy based in Gikondo, Merez 2, Kicukiro District, Kigali, Rwanda. References to “we”, “us”, or “our” mean CodeBridge Academy. References to “this website” mean codebridgecademy.com and its pages.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Training registrations: full name, email address, phone number, gender, location, age group, education and professional status, school or organization, program, preferred duration and schedule, skill level, technologies of interest, learning goals, access to a laptop and internet, motivation, and — where an applicant is a minor — a parent or guardian's name and phone number.
  • Project and quote requests: name, email address, phone number, organization, and the details of the project you describe.
  • Research survey responses (Qualexas): participant information such as name, email, phone number, institution, district, institution type, and occupation, together with the survey answers you choose to submit.
  • Messages to us: anything you include when you contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp, or a website form.

Certificate verification

When you verify a certificate, we receive the certificate number or verification link you submit. The verification result shows only the information needed to confirm a credential: the student's name, program, certificate dates, and completed modules. It does not display email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, registration records, or internal database identifiers.

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting environment records standard technical data such as the requested page, the date and time of the request, the browser user agent, and the IP address. We use this data to keep the site available, diagnose errors, and detect abuse. We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts on this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process training applications, confirm enrolment, and communicate about schedules, fees, and program logistics.
  • Respond to project enquiries, prepare quotes, and deliver software development services.
  • Provide and administer certificate verification for employers and institutions.
  • Carry out the Qualexas research initiative, using responses in aggregated or anonymised form wherever possible.
  • Protect the website, forms, and verification service against spam, fraud, and abuse.
  • Meet accounting, record-keeping, and legal obligations that apply to us in Rwanda.

We rely on your consent when you submit a form, and on our legitimate interest in operating and securing our services. You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that already took place.

3. Student Information

Training records are used for admission, teaching, assessment, certification, and support. Certificate details are kept so that issued certificates can be verified permanently by employers and institutions. Only the limited public fields described above are shown by the verification tool; the rest of your student record remains internal.

4. Survey and Research Information

Participation in the Qualexas survey is voluntary. Survey responses are used to understand skills gaps and workforce readiness, and to improve our programs. Findings may be shared publicly or with partner institutions in aggregated form that does not identify individual respondents. Direct identifiers are not published.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that host the website, store form submissions, or send email on our behalf, and only as needed to provide those services.
  • Trainers, mentors, and staff who need the information to deliver a program or project.
  • Public authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect someone's safety or our legal rights.

6. How Long We Keep Information

  • Training applications and student records: for the duration of the program and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless a longer period is required for certification or legal records.
  • Project enquiries and quotes: for up to 24 months after our last exchange.
  • Survey responses: for up to 24 months, after which they are deleted or retained only in aggregated form.
  • Certificate records: retained for as long as the certificate must remain verifiable, because verification is a permanent service to certificate holders and employers.
  • Server logs: for up to 90 days.

7. How We Protect Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to records, server-side validation of form submissions, parameterised database queries, rate limiting on public forms, and keeping credentials in environment variables rather than in source code. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores a small preference in your browser's local storage so that your light or dark theme choice and a dismissed announcement stay dismissed on future visits. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask us to:

  • confirm what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete information we no longer need;
  • withdraw consent or object to a particular use; or
  • provide a copy of the information you submitted.

Email info.codebridgeacademy@gmail.com with your request. We aim to respond within 30 days. Some records, such as issued certificate details, may be retained in limited form where we need them to keep verification working or to meet a legal obligation.

10. Children and Minors

Some of our programs accept secondary school students. Where an applicant is under 18, the registration form asks for a parent or guardian's contact details, and we expect applications to be submitted with that guardian's knowledge and consent. If you believe a minor's information was submitted without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Links to Other Websites

Our pages link to external services such as Google Play, social media, and maps. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
